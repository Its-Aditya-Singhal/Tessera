# Privacy statement (draft)

- Prompt and chat content stays on your device. Tessera has no servers and sends nothing anywhere.
- Nothing is stored by default. The only thing that will be persisted (from M2) is your settings.
- The undo history for Import lives in the open tab's memory and is gone when you close the tab.
- Diagnostics reports contain selectors, match counts, the site's origin and your browser version.
  They contain no chat text and no conversation URL.
- Chat content is treated as untrusted data: Tessera never follows instructions found inside a
  conversation, and the model is never given tools.
- A "Clear all data" button arrives with settings in M2.
